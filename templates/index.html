Tripmate

Cheap trips, planned for you.

Tell Tripmate where you're flying from, where you want to go, your dates and your budget. It searches real flights, checks whether it fits, and either plans your days or finds you a cheaper way.

  • 1Say the trip: "Dammam to Istanbul, then Dubai, a week each, $1,000"
  • 2It searches flights and adds up the cost
  • 3Within budget: a day-by-day plan. Over: cheaper options

Prices are estimates from the web: always check before you book.
